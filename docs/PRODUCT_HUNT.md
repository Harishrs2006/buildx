# Product Hunt launch kit

Post from the owner's own Product Hunt account (the maker). Everything below is true today; do not add
numbers (users, orders, suppliers) until they are real.

## When
Launch after a few real verified suppliers have products live, so visitors can see real items and prices.
Product Hunt days run 12:01 am to 11:59 pm Pacific time (12:31 pm to 12:29 pm IST next day). Post at the start
of the day for the full 24 hours; Tuesday to Thursday get the most traffic.

## Listing
- **Name:** Sthiva
- **Tagline (max 60):** Construction materials for your site, from verified suppliers
- **Website:** https://sthiva.com
- **Topics:** Marketplace, E-Commerce, Construction (or Real Estate), Made in India / Productivity

**Description (max 260):**
Order cement, steel, sand, bricks and blocks for your building site. Sthiva checks which verified suppliers
deliver to your PIN code, shows one price per item, and you pay cash on delivery. Suppliers and drivers get
their own apps.

## First comment (maker)
Hi Product Hunt, I'm Harish, the maker of Sthiva.

Buying materials for a house in India still means phone calls, price haggling and trucks that arrive late.
Sthiva is a marketplace for construction materials:

- Buyers enter their site PIN and see only what can actually be delivered there, at one clear price.
- Every supplier is verified (GST certificate, shop licence) and confirms stock and prices every week.
- Orders go to one supplier at a time; delivery is by the supplier's own vehicle or a Sthiva driver.
- A 4-digit delivery code means you only hand it over once the material is checked.
- Cash on delivery, no card needed.

There are three apps: Sthiva for buyers, Sthiva Partner for suppliers, and Sthiva Driver for delivery partners,
plus a website for each. We are starting in Karnataka.

I'd love feedback from anyone who has built a house or runs a hardware or building-materials shop.
